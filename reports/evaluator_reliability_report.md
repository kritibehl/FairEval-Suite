# Evaluator Reliability Report

## Summary

| Evaluator | Agreement |
|---|---:|
| Human Reviewers | 0.7333 |
| AI Composite Evaluator | 0.8600 |
| Candidate Self-Evaluation | 0.5800 |

## Findings

- Human reviewers required adjudication for disagreement cases.
- Composite evaluator achieved higher consistency.
- Candidate self-evaluation underperformed on escalation-required cases.

## Recommendation

Use composite evaluator signals plus human review for release decisions.
